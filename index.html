How to play

One deck, one chain, no repeats.

1. Follow the letter. Your word starts with the last letter of the card on the table.

2. Stay in the deck. The word has to exist in the chosen world — Places, Cities, Countries, Movies, Animals or Food. ATLAS checks it as you type.

3. No repeats. Every card used in the match is burned. Type it again and it stays on the table.

4. Beat the clock. Answer before the ring empties. A timeout is a strike; three strikes and you are out.

5. Pass if you are stuck. A pass keeps the letter and costs one of your three passes.

6. Score more. 10 points per card, up to +5 for speed, bonuses for streaks and long names.

7. Rounds. A round ends when every player has played once. The last player standing — or the highest score after the round cap — wins.
